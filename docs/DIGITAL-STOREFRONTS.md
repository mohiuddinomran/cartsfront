# Stack and Academy — CartsFront 1.2.0

The four supplied HTML exports describe desktop/mobile variants of two homepages. Each pair is combined into one responsive, native WordPress block design. No Tailwind, Font Awesome, Google Fonts requests, or export scripts are required.

## Activate a homepage

1. Install `cartsfront-1.2.0.zip` through Appearance → Themes → Add New → Upload Theme. Replace the installed theme when WordPress offers the update.
2. Create an empty page and select **Stack homepage** or **Academy homepage** in its Template setting.
3. Publish the page and choose it under Settings → Reading → A static page.
4. Edit its sections under Appearance → Editor → Templates. Choose the matching optional global style under Styles if desired.

Alternatively, insert the Stack or Academy homepage pattern into page content and choose the existing Storefront landing page template. Do not combine a full homepage pattern with its matching homepage template: that duplicates the design. Automatic product-template selection uses the static front page's assigned homepage template; pattern-only pages do not trigger it.

## Connect your products and plans

- Create actual digital products, prices, billing intervals, and purchase options in FluentCart. The live catalog section uses FluentCart's existing products shortcode; it reflects published products and actual catalog prices. Configure catalog selection with plugin-supported controls if the store also contains physical goods.
- The pricing cards are editable presentation blocks, not live price data. Their sample prices and benefits are labeled as examples. Replace all prices, benefits, site limits, renewal/access terms, and text with your real offer before publishing.
- Edit every plan button to the corresponding product page or the purchase URL generated by your configured commerce plugin. Initially, buttons scroll to the available-products section and do not select or charge for a plan.
- Set up payment gateways and subscriptions in your commerce plugin. Configure software licensing, updates, course delivery, membership access, and transactional email with the appropriate plugins and their supported integrations. These features are not implemented by the theme.
- Test each linked plan, billing interval, checkout, access delivery, renewal/cancellation policy, and email on your actual store before launch. No live store configuration or payment transactions were changed in this release.

## Academy content

Replace the three instructor placeholders with real names, biographies, and licensed images. Replace the suggested learning path with your curriculum. Add your own video to the Video block in the sample-lesson section; the section is hidden on the public page until a video source is present. It remains editable in the Site Editor. No fake play button is included.

## Product pages and assets

Stack and Academy product templates reuse FluentCart's native gallery, price, purchasing, and review blocks. An unassigned product uses the matching design when the static front page has the corresponding homepage template. Individually assigned product templates are preserved.

The package contains original GPL SVG illustrations instead of the supplied exports' remotely hosted images. Replace them in the editor with images you have rights to use. Academy uses a system serif font; Stack uses the already bundled OFL font. Existing Site Editor customizations remain authoritative; the new templates do not overwrite them.
